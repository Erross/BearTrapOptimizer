Step 3

Your materials and speedups

Enter what you own now. Reserves are protected. Gear materials and forgehammers are spent only through the upgrades calculated from your recorded gear. For other items, tick “Can use” only when you can spend them in the game.

For ascensions or other fixed costs, set the smallest usable batch and, if needed, an overall usable limit. Speedups score only against the timer capacity you enter in step 5. An unopened random chest is not a guaranteed material.

Step 4

Store balances and available offers

The same coins cannot be spent twice. Enter your balances and reserves, then enable the offers that are available to you. Check prices, pack sizes and remaining purchases against your shop; age, discounts and earlier purchases can change them.

Expected currency income before each day

Optional. Enter only income you expect to receive before spending that day. Zero means no extra income.

Available shop offers

One row covers the remaining stock of one offer. For a future reset, add another row with its own first available day and stock limit. Offers default to off until you confirm them. Gem purchases also require an enabled offer.

Step 5

Daily points and targets

Targets default to 200,000 personal points per day; edit them for your event. “Expected other points” is for intel, gathering or other activities outside this inventory. Do not include the upgrades or speedups this planner will already count.

Timers you can actually finish

Enter the remaining timer minutes you can speed through on each day, after your buffs and helps. General speedups can fill these timers too. Zero excludes that activity.

Day 4 troop-production points require the troop batch details below. Using a training speedup does not itself score on day 4 in the reference rules.

Add a training or promotion batch

Enter a complete batch and its remaining timer. Promotion earns the difference between the two troop tiers. Each batch can finish only once.

Scoring rules and reference data

The editable reference pack is community data, not a game API. Confirm your event’s scoring and shop stock before spending. Normal enhancement XP, unknown gear, unentered pet advancement and random chest outcomes do not earn assumed points. Gear coverage currently ends at Red T2 3★, charms at level 21; do not select a lower level to represent more advanced gear.
