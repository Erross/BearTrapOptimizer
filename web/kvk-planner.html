Step 2

What is your current gear?

Record each piece’s current color, tier and stars, plus its three charm levels. The planner calculates the next upgrades and their material requirements. Not sure? Leave it as “Not entered”. That piece will be left out of the plan.

Optional: progress between full levels

Only use this if your upgrade screen actually shows those steps. For example, charm level 5 with 2/5 progress toward level 6 means choose level 5 and enter 2 steps done. These steps are separate from gear stars. Leave this off if your screen only shows whole levels.

Step 4

What can you buy?

First, enter your store currency balances. Leave unused stores at zero. Then choose a store below and tick the items you can buy. You are allowing options, not buying anything yet.

Step 5

What score are you aiming for?

The usual target is filled in: 200,000 points each day. Change it if your event is different. If you have already started, open that day’s extra options. “Other activities” means intel, gathering or points this planner won’t calculate; don’t count the same points twice.

Using speedups? Add the timers you can finish

How many timer minutes can you use each day?

Enter the remaining minutes on buildings, research and training you can actually start, after buffs and helps. For example, a 2-hour building timer is 120 minutes. This is work you can speed up, not the speedups in your bag. Zero means no work available.

Day 4 troop-production points require the troop batch details below. Using a training speedup does not itself score on day 4 in the reference rules.

Add a training or promotion batch

Enter a complete batch and its remaining timer. Promotion earns the difference between the two troop tiers. Each batch can finish only once.

Advanced: scoring rules and calculation limits

The editable reference pack is community data, not a game API. Confirm your event’s scoring and shop stock before spending. Normal enhancement XP, unknown gear, unentered pet advancement and random chest outcomes do not earn assumed points. Gear coverage currently ends at Red T2 3★, charms at level 21; do not select a lower level to represent more advanced gear.
