Your own rallyThe rally you start.

A joinA march you send to someone else’s rally.

1 Before Bear

Save your troop setup

Open Optimize Me. Enter your troops, march limits and heroes. Save the counts it gives you.

Use the shown first hero for each join. If it says No hero, remove all heroes from that march.

A troop cap is a limit. You do not need to reach it.

2 At the start

Join as soon as you can

Send all your joining marches as soon as there are rallies with room. Use your saved troop counts and stay within the alliance’s troop cap.

If there is no room yet, wait for another rally to open. You do not need to hold back for other players.

Have to wait a minute for space? That is fine.

3 If you start rallies

Start with your group

Use your group’s start time and the own-rally troops and heroes in your setup. Join-only players do not start rallies.

A small own march can leave more troops for joins. It is not best for everyone. Use the tool to compare a small and a full own march.

Keep your own-rally troops and heroes ready for your start.

4 Through the whole Bear

Get home. Go again.

Join a rally with room that your troops can reach before it leaves. If several work, pick the one leaving soonest.

As each march gets home, send it again. You can join rallies from any group. Do not wait for all your marches to return.

Restart your own rally when it returns, unless the organizer gives a repeat time.

Waves, without the confusion

A wave is a group of rally starters

Groups start one minute apart. This gives returning troops more rallies to join.

  1. Group 10:00Start
  2. Group 21:00Start
  3. Group 32:00Start
  4. Group 43:00Start

Example only: time since Bear started. Use your organizer’s group times.

Your group tells you when to start. It does not limit who you join.

You do not need all your joining marches to fit in one wave. Troops hit, return, then join another rally.

Two quick checks

A few troops left over?

Equal joining presets can leave a few troops after rounding. Add them to your own rally in game if it has room. Keep your joining presets the same.

Bear is nearly over?

The rally must hit before Bear ends. Leave time for its timer and the trip to Bear. With a 5-minute timer, start before 25:00 and allow extra time for travel.
