What these numbers mean

Each successful twice-daily alliance refresh archives power, Town Center levels and the latest available shared troop submissions. Repeated copies of the same observation are saved once.

Troops are the counts players entered for Bear, including any separately entered lower-tier own-rally reserve. They are self-reported and can become stale. T8–T10 totals exclude that lower-tier reserve. An individual troop point is dated when the player saved it; alliance totals are dated when they were captured, using the latest available submissions. For individuals, its capture time shows when it entered this archive.

Alliance power is the total reported by MightPulse. Alliance troop totals add up entered counts for current members only; they are not an estimate for the full alliance. Every point shows reporting coverage. Failed reads retain labelled last-known submissions. Lines and growth percentages stop across changes in reporting members or the roster, because those totals cannot be compared as troop growth.

Power history includes recovered snapshots from 16 and 21 October 2026. Troop history begins with the first captured submission. Changes overwritten between captures cannot be recovered. Missing submissions or failed reads do not become zero.
