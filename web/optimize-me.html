Heroes and your own rally

Tick heroes you actually have and check their first Expedition skill level. Saved profiles may show only some of your heroes. Level 0 means unknown or not unlocked.

Who gets the best troops: joins or my own rally?

Use the combined plan to search for a better total across all your joins and your own march, using the same troop inventory. It uses the damage-reference formation policy. With no measurements entered, it assumes equal rally strength and equal hit counts; add your measurements below to personalize it. Your current packet stays unchanged until you choose a plan.

Small uses the weakest entered tier, or the lower-tier troops you have explicitly confirmed. Full compares single-tier own packets using your chosen formation policy because the own-rally entry supports one tier. Your leader heroes stay reserved in both.

Which wins for my rallies? Add comparable damage and hit counts

Use the same test formation and troop count when comparing your own rally with the rallies you normally join. Enter the number of hits each march can complete during Bear.

The combined search moves troops between your own march, identical joins and unused stock, then compares their total per-type square-root reference score using your same-formation damage ratio and completed hits. This is an integer numerical search, not a guarantee of the global optimum or actual in-game damage. It assumes that ratio applies across the compared packets. Lower-tier dross is credited zero damage. It does not model the rally space displaced for other players. Measurements stay on this device; the chosen packet saves with your setup.
