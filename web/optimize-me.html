Your personal plan

How this estimate works

Calculated mix balances diminishing returns across troop types using a community damage formula. It uses entered leader bonuses, or equal bonuses if blank, and includes archer counter and average Volley. Mixed tiers use attack-weighted counts. Unentered hero effects, Truegold skills and the other troops in a rally can change the best mix.

Archers first fills archers, then cavalry, then infantry. Target ratio uses your percentages and fills shortages in that same order. All options use the strongest available tiers within each troop type and respect your inventory and march limits.
